How do I redeem my points?

Once you've earned enough points to exchange for a reward, you can redeem them at any time by signing in to Ipsos iSay and clicking on the Rewards tab at the top of the page. Here you'll be able to browse the Rewards Catalogue to see the available rewards and the amount of points needed. Select the reward you would like to redeem and follow the steps to place your order. All rewards will be sent to the email address associated with your Ipsos iSay account.

What can I redeem my points for?

You can redeem your points for a variety of great rewards, including digital gift cards and charitable donations, such as the Ipsos Foundation, which promotes access to education for disadvantaged children and youth. Browse our Rewards Catalogue by clicking on the Rewards tab at the top of the page.

Digital gift cards, prepaid cards and PayPal funds are delivered instantly to the email address associated with your Ipsos iSay account. Please check each reward description on the Rewards page for estimated delivery times. For instant rewards, make sure to claim your reward within 2 weeks after redemption if you need us to resend the code because it did not arrive or if you cannot use it.

Do my points ever expire?

Your points don't expire as long as you remain an active member of Ipsos iSay.

If you terminate your membership, if your membership becomes inactive, or if you're deactivated, your points account will be closed and any unused points will be forfeited.

How long do I have to wait to redeem rewards after I join?

Newly registered Ipsos iSay members will need to wait 96 hours from their registration date before redeeming rewards. If you try to order any reward during this waiting period, you'll receive an error message at checkout.

Please note that, during this period, we will evaluate your survey answers and behaviours. According to Ipsos iSay Terms and Conditions we are entitled to terminate a membership to Ipsos iSay for any bad behaviour, fraudulent use or failure to abide by the terms and conditions, policies, or survey rules. In the event of termination, all points and any rewards held in "Pending" status will be forfeited.

How long do I have to wait to redeem rewards after I change my email address?

Ipsos iSay members who request an email change will need to wait 48 hours from the date of change before redeeming rewards. If you try to order any reward during this waiting period, you'll receive an error message at checkout.

Please note that, during this period, we will evaluate your survey answers and behaviours. According to Ipsos iSay Terms and Conditions we are entitled to terminate a membership to Ipsos iSay for any bad behaviour, fraudulent use or failure to abide by the terms and conditions, policies, or survey rules. In the event of termination, all points and any rewards held in "Pending" status will be forfeited.

I wanted to redeem points for a reward, but it no longer appears on the Rewards page. What can I do?

When a reward is out of stock, it becomes unavailable automatically. Don't worry, we're likely already working to restock it and it should be available again in a few days. We suggest checking the Rewards page periodically.

I redeemed my points for a reward but haven't received anything on my email. What can I do?

All rewards will be sent to the email address associated with your Ipsos iSay account. If the reward is not in your inbox, please also check your Spam folder. If the reward is not in your Spam folder either - Contact support.

What is Cash to Bank Transfer & How Do I Redeem?

Cash to Bank Transfer is a secure, convenient way to turn your Ipsos iSay points into real money sent directly to your bank account.

How to redeem:

Select the Cash Payout option in the Rewards page and confirm your redemption.

Click the activation link sent to your email.

Enter your correct banking details and submit your request.

All set! Your cash payout order is now being processed.

What information do I need to get the cash payout to my bank account?

To complete the cash to bank transfer, you will need to provide:

Account holder name: (Must match your registered Ipsos iSay profile name).

IBAN / Account Number: Depending on your region.

How long does the cash payout take?

Bank transfers typically take between 1 to 2 business days to arrive in your account. Standard bank holidays or weekends may cause slight delays.

Is there a fee associated with the cash payout?

No, Ipsos iSay does not charge any fees for cash-to-bank transfers. You receive the full value of your redeemed points.

Can I edit or cancel my cash payout request after submitting it?

No. Once submitted, processing starts immediately. Cash payout requests cannot be edited, canceled, refunded, or exchanged. Always double-check your banking details before confirming!

What if my payment is delayed or missing?

If your payment hasn't arrived after 7 business days, check with your bank first. If they have no record of an incoming transfer, contact our Support team via the FAQ/Contact Us page.

(Note: The currency of your bank account must match the local currency of your registered profile country).
