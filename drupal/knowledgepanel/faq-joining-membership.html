Joining and Membership

Back to Index

Who can join?

KnowledgePanel is by invitation only. Only households that Ipsos has randomly selected and contacted (by letter or phone) can join. If you did not receive an invitation, you cannot sign up at this time.

Why was I invited?

Your household was chosen at random from lists of phone numbers or postal addresses in your country. This helps make sure our surveys include all kinds of people, not just those who volunteer online. That is why your participation is especially important.

Can I share my invitation with someone else?

No. Your invitation is personal to your household and cannot be shared with friends, neighbours or relatives. Only the households that Ipsos has directly invited can join KnowledgePanel.

Can I stop being a member whenever I want?

Yes. You can leave the panel at any time, without giving a reason. After you leave, we will stop inviting you to surveys, and you can ask us to delete your data in line with our Privacy Policy.

Do you have more questions?

If you didn't find the answer, reach out for help, and we'll respond shortly.
