How many surveys will I be invited to?

On average, you will receive about 2-3 survey invitations per month. The exact number can vary depending on the studies running at any given time and how closely your household matches what a study needs.

How long does each survey take?

Most surveys take between 10 and 15 minutes to complete. The estimated time is always shown before you start, so you can decide whether it is a good moment to take part.

Can I skip a survey I'm not interested in?

Yes. Taking part is always voluntary. You can skip any survey you receive without any impact on your membership or your ability to receive future invitations.

What happens if I don't complete a survey?

Nothing negative happens. You can leave a survey unfinished at any time. If it is important for a study, we may send you a reminder, but you are always free to decide whether to continue.

Do you have more questions?

If you didn't find the answer, reach out for help, and we'll respond shortly.
