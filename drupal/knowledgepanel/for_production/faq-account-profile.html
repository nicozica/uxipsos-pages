How do I update my personal details?

You can update details such as your address, phone number or household information at any time from your account settings. Keeping this information current helps us send you surveys that are relevant to you.

I forgot my password, what should I do?

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page to reset it. If you no longer have access to the email linked to your account, contact our panel support team and we will help you regain access.

Can I change my email address?

Yes. You can update your email address from your profile at any time. We will send a confirmation message to your new address to verify the change before it takes effect.

How do I close my account?

You can leave KnowledgePanel at any time from your account settings, or by contacting our support team. Once you leave, we will stop inviting you to surveys and you can ask us to delete your data.

Do you have more questions?

If you didn't find the answer, reach out for help, and we'll respond shortly.
