Is this real or a scam?

This is a genuine research panel run by Ipsos, an independent research company. If you are unsure, you can check Ipsos on our main website, or use the contact details in your invitation or on this site to speak to us directly.

Who is Ipsos?

Ipsos is an independent research company that conducts surveys in France and around the world on topics such as health, work, education, transport and public services.

We work with public organisations, universities, charities, the media and companies to understand people's views and experiences. We follow strict professional rules and data protection laws.

What if I have technical problems or need help?

If you have trouble with a survey, your account, or anything else, you can contact our panel support team by email or phone. Their contact details are shown on this website and in your invitation.
