How is my personal data used?

Your answers are used only for research. They are stored securely and kept separate from your name and contact details, and are never used for marketing or advertising.

Are my answers shared with anyone?

Results are only published in anonymous or grouped form, for example "X% of people think that...". Your individual answers are never shared or sold to third parties.

Can I ask KnowledgePanel to delete my data?

Yes. You can ask us to remove your data from the panel at any time, whether or not you decide to keep taking part in surveys. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does KnowledgePanel comply with GDPR?

Yes. We comply with data protection laws such as GDPR and with the rules of local authorities and professional research codes in every country where KnowledgePanel operates.

Do you have more questions?

If you didn't find the answer, reach out for help, and we'll respond shortly.
