Points, rewards and redeeming

Back to Index

How do I earn points?

You earn points every time you complete a survey. Completing your welcome profile survey also gives you a starting reward, and some studies may offer bonus points depending on their length.

What can I exchange my points for?

Points can be exchanged for a range of rewards, including gift cards and other options shown in your account. Survey rewards depend on length, usually worth €1-2 for a 10-15 minute survey.

Is there a minimum number of points to redeem a reward?

Yes, most rewards have a minimum points threshold before they can be redeemed. You can check the exact requirements for each reward from your rewards page.

How long does it take to receive my reward?

Most rewards are processed within a few business days of your request. Delivery times can vary slightly depending on the reward type you choose.

Do you have more questions?

If you didn't find the answer, reach out for help, and we'll respond shortly.
